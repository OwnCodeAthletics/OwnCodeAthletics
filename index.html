Own Code Athletics exists to remove barriers and create real opportunities for athletes of all ages who are willing to put in the work.

Our Mission

Too many athletes fall through the cracks not due to lack of talent, but because of lacking access or guidance and opportunity. Youth athletics lack mentorship and exposure. Skilled athletes miss opportunities due to financial or structural barriers while adult athletes are often overlooked, despite continued driving potential. OCA creates pathways for athletes to develop, compete and grow through training and development, support, mentorship and guidance. OCA grants access to opportunities, resources, and exposure. OCA supports youth athletes seeking direction and confidence, competitive athletes working toward the next level and adult athletes still chasing their dreams. Talent is everywhere, but opportunity is not. OCA closes that gap by aiding athletes willing to do the work.
